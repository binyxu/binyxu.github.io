Exposes working-state signals through a typed, addressable context layer and a context-tool action space. On 75 long-horizon tasks, VISTA raises Gemini-3-Flash success from 22.7% to 50.7% and complements GRPO post-training.

From Multi-Agent to Single-Agent: When Is Skill Distillation Beneficial?

Binyan Xu, Dong Fang, Haitao Li, Kehuan Zhang
